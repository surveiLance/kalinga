Background
Project Kalinga is a human-centered co-design initiative where educators collaboratively solve mental
health promotion challenges in last-mile schools. It equips teachers to co-design practical, locally
relevant solutions while strengthening peer learning, leadership, and community ownership rather
than relying on top-down compliance.
In June 2026, a three-day co-design workshop was conducted with teachers from last-mile schools
(LMS) in Agusan del Sur to identify priority needs and design solutions that address their mental
health and wellbeing challenges.

App Details
Target users: Last-mile school teachers (for now, in Agusan del Sur)
Platform: Web and mobile, with offline accessibility necessary for last-mile use
*Core Functionality:
Record: Assistance in minor record-keeping (attendance, marking/grading, etc.)
Create: Multi-grade lesson planning support (AI-supported lesson plan builder up from
competency to lesson plan to activity sheets)
Share: Shared resource repository with upload, search by context (e.g., indigenous communities,
multigrade combinations), tagging, customization features
Collaborate: Teacher collaboration and knowledge exchange
*can change after teacher consultations
